      Frequently asked questions

      Why do gel nails require maintenance?

      1. Natural growth Your nails grow an average of 3mm per month. After 2–3 weeks, a gap appears between the gel and the cuticle. The look is no longer flawless, and the space can collect dirt and bacteria. 2. Loss of adhesion Over time, the gel may begin to lift slightly due to activities like washing dishes, using cleaning products, or accidental knocks. If you don’t get a fill (maintenance), water can seep underneath, potentially leading to fungal growth (that green spot no one wants) or causing the nail to break. 3. Balance and strength Gel nails are designed with a specific balance point, known as the "apex." As the nail grows out, the weight shifts toward the tip, making it more prone to breaking or cracking in the middle—which can be painful.

        How long can my nails last?

        Ideal timing in Toronto: Gel: every 2–3 weeks; Fiber: every 3–4 weeks (it lasts longer, which is why Brazilians love it). Canada’s cold, dry climate causes more dehydration, so maintenance sessions also help with hydration.

          What are the advantages of having gel or fiber nails?

          FIBERGLASS - the most requested option in Canada: More natural look: The fine fiberglass strands are ultra-thin, making them look just like your own nails. Highly durable: Won’t easily break, even in Toronto’s -20°C cold. Long-lasting (30+ days): Perfect for those with a busy lifestyle. Great for length: Ideal if you’ve bitten your nails or want long, almond-shaped nails. Strong adhesion: Doesn’t lift or peel easily. GEL - for that mirror-like shine: Long-lasting shine: Stays looking freshly done for 3 weeks. Faster application: Takes 1.5 hours, compared to 2.5 hours for fiberglass. Less initial prep: Requires less filing. Versatile: Perfect for "baby boomer" (ombré) styles, French manicures, and encapsulated designs. Easier removal: Comes off quickly whenever you’re ready to take it off.
